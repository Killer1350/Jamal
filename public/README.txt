Jamal's Grill - Custom Menu Images Folder
=========================================

You can drag and drop your own restaurant images into this "public" folder!

Supported filenames (examples):
- /my_shawarma.jpg
- /legend_platter.png
- /jambu_rice.webp

How to apply them to your menu items:
1. Open the "Jamal's Grill Control Center" (Admin panel in top-right of the header)
2. Enter the admin passcode (default: admin123)
3. Under the "Configure Custom Menu" tab, click "Edit" on any menu item
4. For the "Custom Image URL or Local Filename" field, enter: /YOUR_FILENAME.jpg (e.g. `/my_shawarma.jpg`)
5. Save, and your image will show up on the website instantly!
